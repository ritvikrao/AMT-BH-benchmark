What changed
------------
Each step decomposes from scratch in ~24 histogram rounds (binary tree: one
level per round), each a barrier: PE 0 picks bins to split, broadcasts,
every PE refines and contributes, reduction back to PE 0. Every round, every
PE re-added each unsplit bin and re-summed its particles' cost, a pass over
all ~33k local particles, even under -balance=count where nothing reads the
sum. Now an unsplit bin keeps last round's descriptor, and costs are summed
only under -balance=cost. The decomposition itself is unchanged.
