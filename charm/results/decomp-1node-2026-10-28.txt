6. Not started, by decision (Kale, 2026-10-28 evening)
   - Process-shared tree cache (paratreet2 treecache library) or direct
     reads of co-located PEs' trees: equivalent on one process (no fetch
     either way; each PE adds its nodes to the common tree, sets moments
     upward, then a process-level barrier before any walk starts, so no walk
     waits for an in-process node). The shared-tree structure is the one to
     adopt when multi-node runs need it: it also removes duplicate fetches
     among the PEs of a process. Worth measuring first: distinct requested
     keys per process vs total requests, in one 8x15 run.
   - Multi-level fetch depth (-chunkdepth) and the octree: Ritvik/Aditya.
     Note the reply sizes: binary depth 4 = 2+4+8+16 = 30 nodes (4.8 KB),
     octree depth 2 = 8+64 = 72 nodes (11.5 KB); the requested node itself
     is not in the reply.
   - Stealing stays within a process; no multi-node stealing experiments now.
