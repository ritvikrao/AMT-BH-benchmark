== 1. upstream main reproduces the report (4M, 4 PEs)
  step 0 E_P -2.822962506e+20
  step 1 E_P +8.109324964e+22

main + a count of particles left at potential 0 after the traversal:

These are particles whose TreePiece the traversal skipped (MULTINODE.md,
Step 1; fixed in 77a8a5a, not upstream). Each adds +G m^2/(2 eps) through the
self-term correction, which at eps = 1e-11 is ~7e17 per body: 9e4 bodies
give the +8.1e22. With eps = 0.05 the same fault showed as spikes of ~4e-3.

== 2. charm-multinode before the change: no untouched particles, but E_P
depends on the layout (4M, step 0)
  4 PEs  -2.822962506e+20   (and a jump of 7.7e-5 at step 3)
  16 PEs -2.827564934e+20
  64 PEs -2.827646634e+20
The kernel added each particle's self term -m/eps and kickDriftKick
subtracted it again. A galaxy's central black hole has ~10x the mass of its
disk; its m/eps is ~6e38 (kernel units, G factored out), with an ulp of
~7e22, above every single contribution from another body (~1e20). Those round
away in an order that depends on the layout. Accelerations are not affected.
With eps = 0 the self term is m/0: "NaN accel detected" at step 0.
